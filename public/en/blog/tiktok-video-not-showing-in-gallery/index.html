TikTok Video Not Showing in Gallery? 6 Fixes

2026-11-01 · UnduhTik

Downloaded a TikTok video, but it is not showing in your gallery? The file may be saved somewhere other than your gallery. Here are six common causes and practical fixes for Android and iPhone.

1. The video is in the Downloads folder, not the gallery

Files downloaded through a browser usually go to the Download folder and do not appear in the gallery app on their own. Open your Files app, go to Download and sort by date to find the newest file.

2. The gallery has not refreshed

Sometimes the file exists but the gallery has not scanned it yet. Close the gallery app completely and open it again, or restart your phone.

3. The file needs to be moved to a video folder

If the video shows up in the Files app but not in the gallery, copy it to a folder your gallery scans, such as Movies or DCIM. Folder names vary by phone, so use the folder where your other videos are saved.

4. The download was incomplete or the file is damaged

If the file is very small or will not play, the download was cut off. Delete it and download it again on a stable connection: copy the video link from TikTok's Share button, paste it into UnduhTik and tap Download.

5. Your phone storage is almost full

When storage is nearly full, files may not save properly. Free up some space and download the video again.

6. On iPhone: check the Files app and save manually

On iPhone, downloads from Safari usually go to the Files app. Open the video, tap the Share icon and choose Save Video to add it to Photos.

How to avoid this next time

  • Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, not the browser that opens inside the TikTok or Instagram app.
  • Right after downloading, open the Download folder to check that the file is there and plays.
