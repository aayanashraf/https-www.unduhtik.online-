How to Download TikTok Videos on PC Without Any App

2026-11-02 · UnduhTik

Want to save a TikTok video on your laptop or PC without installing anything? You can do it entirely in your browser, on Windows, Mac or Linux. Here is how, step by step.

Step by step

  1. Open the video on tiktok.com in your browser and copy the full address from the address bar.
  2. Open UnduhTik, paste the link into the box and click Download.
  3. Choose what you need: the video without watermark, or the audio as MP3.
  4. Open your Downloads folder to find the file. In Chrome or Edge on Windows, press Ctrl+J to see the downloads list.

If the download does not work

  • Make sure the video is public and the link is complete, with no extra text around it.
  • Try again after a moment, or refresh the page and paste the link again.
  • Turn off a VPN or ad blocker temporarily if it blocks the download.
  • Use an up-to-date version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
