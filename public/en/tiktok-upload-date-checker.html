How It Works

A TikTok video ID stores the creation time in its upper bits (Unix time). This tool reads it and converts it to a readable date and time. Everything runs in your browser, the link is not sent to our server.

How to Use It

  1. Open the video on TikTok web and copy the full address from the address bar (it looks like tiktok.com/@user/video/number).
  2. Paste it in the box above and click Check Date.
  3. See the result in your device time and in UTC.

Why Short Links Don't Work

Short links such as vt.tiktok.com do not contain the video ID. Open the link in your browser first, wait for the video page to load, then copy the full address.

Is the result always accurate?

The tool reads the time embedded in the ID, which usually matches when the video was created. For re-uploaded or edited videos the date may differ from what the app shows.
