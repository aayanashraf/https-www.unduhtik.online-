Download Multiple TikTok Videos at Once

Paste up to 10 TikTok links in one box. UnduhTik processes them one by one and shows all the results in a single list.

    How to use it

    1. Copy the links of several TikTok videos and paste them all in the box above, one link per line.
    2. Click Process All Links and wait. Links are processed one at a time to keep the service stable.
    3. Each video appears in the list with Download Video and MP3 buttons. With two or more videos you also get a Download all videos button.

    Good to know

    • Up to 10 links at a time.
    • Only public videos can be processed. Private or deleted videos fail and are marked in the list.
    • If a link is a photo slideshow, a button opens it on the main page.
    • Download all videos starts several downloads one after another. Your browser may ask for permission to download multiple files, so choose Allow.
    • The list is not stored on our servers and disappears when you close the page.

    FAQ

    Why one at a time?
    So the service is not overwhelmed and results stay stable for everyone.

    Is it free?
    Yes, with no sign-up.

    For a single video, use the UnduhTik main page. Videos belong to their creators, so use them for personal purposes and ask for permission before sharing them again.

    Good to know

    • Up to 10 links at a time. If you paste more, the first 10 are processed and the rest are ignored. You will see a notice when this happens.
    • One link after another. Each link is processed in turn with a short pause between them, so a batch of 10 takes a little while. Keep the page open until the list shows every result.
    • Automatic retry. If the server is busy, the tool waits and tries again a few times before showing an error for that link.
    • Photo slideshows. A slideshow shows an "Open photos" button instead of a video download, because its photos are saved from the single-post page.
    • Video and MP3 buttons. Each video result shows a Download Video button (without watermark when available) and an MP3 button when audio is available. File names include the video title and its position in the list.

    Tips for best results

    Paste one link per line. Check that each video is public, because private videos cannot be processed. If a link fails, copy it again from the TikTok app and paste it on its own line. Large batches work best on a stable Wi-Fi connection.

    Use the files responsibly

    Videos belong to their creators. Use the downloaded files for personal viewing, and ask the creator's permission before reposting or using them commercially. For copyright concerns, see our copyright notice.
