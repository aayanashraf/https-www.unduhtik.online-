Why use a browser on your PC

Many people want to save a TikTok video to a laptop or desktop but do not want to install another app. A browser-based downloader solves this. You open the site, paste the link, and save the file to your computer. Nothing needs to be installed, and the same steps work on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Step by step on a PC or laptop

  1. Open the TikTok app or website, find the video, and copy its link.
  2. Open UnduhTik in English in your browser.
  3. Paste the link into the box and start the download.
  4. Choose the format you need: the version without watermark, the version with watermark, or MP3 for audio only.
  5. The file is saved to your Downloads folder by default. You can move it anywhere you like.

Choosing the right format

Pick the version without watermark when you want a clean copy to watch again. Pick the version with watermark if you need to show where the video came from. Pick MP3 when you only need the sound. The file size is usually smaller for audio, so it is a good choice when storage is limited.

Checking the file after download

Open the file in your usual video player to confirm it plays correctly. If the video does not open, or the file is much smaller than expected, delete it and download it again with a fresh copy of the link.

Common problems on a computer

If the download fails, first check that the video is public and that the whole link was copied. A slow or unstable internet connection can also stop a large file. Try again after a few minutes, or switch to a different network. If you still have trouble, read the guide on videos that do not show up for more causes and fixes.

Respect the creator

Videos belong to their creators. Use the downloaded file for personal viewing. If you want to repost it, use it in a project, or use it for business, ask the creator for permission first. Our copyright notice explains how to report content that should not be shared.
