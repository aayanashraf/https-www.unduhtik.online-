Good to know

  • Up to 10 links at a time. If you paste more, the first 10 are processed and the rest are ignored. You will see a notice when this happens.
  • One link after another. Each link is processed in turn with a short pause between them, so a batch of 10 takes a little while. Keep the page open until the list shows every result.
  • Automatic retry. If the server is busy, the tool waits and tries again a few times before showing an error for that link.
  • Photo slideshows. A slideshow shows an "Open photos" button instead of a video download, because its photos are saved from the single-post page.
  • Video and MP3 buttons. Each video result shows a Download Video button (without watermark when available) and an MP3 button when audio is available. File names include the video title and its position in the list.

Tips for best results

Paste one link per line. Check that each video is public, because private videos cannot be processed. If a link fails, copy it again from the TikTok app and paste it on its own line. Large batches work best on a stable Wi-Fi connection.
